About the Series

The Appalachian Sniper Series is a regional rimfire precision rifle competition series focused on practical long-range shooting skills. We emphasize fair competition, good sportsmanship, and bringing together shooters from across the Appalachian region.

Rules & Equipment

  • Rimfire rifles (.22 LR primarily)
  • Open and Production divisions (adjust as needed)
  • Standard PRS/NRL22-style stage rules with series-specific adjustments
